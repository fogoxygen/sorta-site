Sorta Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

1. Overview

Sorta is a personal note-taking and organization app. It can process information that you choose to enter or provide, including note content, categories, importance, schedules, reminders, place names, voice transcripts, and explicitly selected images.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

In the current beta, notes and most app settings are primarily stored on your device. This can include note content, category, importance, schedules, reminders, place names, favorite places, and settings.

Sorta does not currently operate its own cloud-sync backend for storing users’ complete note database. Cloud Sync is not currently available. If future versions add cloud sync, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

3. Voice Input & Speech Recognition

Voice Input works only when you actively choose to use it and may request Microphone and Speech Recognition permission. Sorta uses Apple Speech Recognition technologies. Depending on your device, language, operating system, and Apple service availability, speech recognition may be processed on-device or through Apple services.

Sorta does not intentionally retain raw microphone recordings as part of your saved note data. If you choose to save a result, the transcript and structured note fields may be stored on your device.

4. Photos & Camera

The Camera is used only when you actively choose to take a photo. When you choose a photo, Sorta uses the Apple system photo picker and receives only the content you explicitly select. Sorta does not scan or read your entire photo library.

Image-based automatic organization is limited beta functionality and is not generally available.

5. Location & Nearby Places

Location permission is optional. It is used when you ask Sorta to find nearby places or help fill a Place field. Location requests are user-initiated; Sorta does not continuously track your location or build a location history.

Nearby place results use Apple MapKit and Apple Maps services. Approximate Location can be used, although results may be less precise. Favorite place names may be stored on your device. Sorta does not store a precise GPS history as part of your notes.

6. Notifications & Reminders

Sorta uses iOS Local Notifications for reminders. Delivery can depend on notification permission, device settings, system state, and iOS behavior. Sorta does not guarantee reminder delivery and should not be relied on as an emergency, medical, legal, or safety-critical alert system.

7. AI Features

Current beta AI-related experiences may use local, rule-based, mock, or demo logic. AI Sort and automatic image organization are not generally available. The current beta does not operate a production workflow that sends your complete note database to a third-party AI provider.

The Allow Online AI setting is a control prepared for future functionality; turning it on does not currently send your notes to OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic, or another third-party AI provider. If a future version introduces external AI processing, this policy will be updated to explain what data is sent, why it is sent, which providers are involved, and how applicable user controls are used.

8. Apple & System Services

When you use relevant features, Apple system services such as Apple Speech Recognition, Core Location, MapKit and Apple Maps, Photos Picker, Camera APIs, and UserNotifications may process information according to Apple’s own technologies and privacy practices. Sorta does not control how Apple processes information through those services.

9. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell users’ personal note data. Sorta currently does not operate a server that stores users’ complete note database.

The current beta does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

10. Data Retention & Deletion

Local data is generally kept on your device until you delete a note, delete the app, or clear related app data. Undo is a short-lived in-app recovery mechanism, not a cloud backup. The developer cannot restore deleted local notes from a server.

11. Security

Sorta relies on iOS application sandboxing and device-level protections for local app data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

12. Children’s Privacy

Sorta is a general-purpose productivity app and is not specifically directed to children.

13. Changes to This Policy

This policy may change as Sorta evolves. If future features such as cloud sync, external AI processing, advertising, or analytics materially affect data handling, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Sorta 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 12 日

1. 概述

Sorta 是一款个人笔记与整理应用。它可能处理你主动输入或提供的信息，包括笔记内容、分类、重要性、日程、提醒、地点名称、语音转写内容，以及你明确选择的图片。

2. 存储在设备上的信息

在当前 Beta 版本中，笔记和大多数应用设置主要存储在你的设备上，可能包括笔记内容、分类、重要性、日程、提醒、地点名称、收藏地点和设置。

Sorta 目前不运营用于存储用户完整笔记数据库的自有云同步后端。云同步目前尚不可用；如未来版本提供云同步，我们会相应更新本政策。

3. 语音输入与语音识别

语音输入仅在你主动使用时才会工作，并可能请求麦克风和语音识别权限。Sorta 使用 Apple 语音识别技术。语音识别可能在设备上或通过 Apple 服务处理，具体取决于设备、语言、操作系统及 Apple 服务可用性。

Sorta 不会有意将原始麦克风录音作为已保存笔记数据长期保留。如果你选择保存结果，转写内容和结构化笔记字段可能存储在你的设备上。

4. 照片与相机

相机仅在你主动选择拍照时使用。选择照片时，Sorta 使用 Apple 系统照片选择器，并且只会接收你明确选择的内容；Sorta 不会扫描或读取你的整个照片图库。

基于图片的自动整理目前属于有限 Beta 功能，尚未全面开放。

5. 位置与附近地点

位置权限是可选的，仅在你要求 Sorta 查找附近地点或协助填写地点字段时使用。定位请求由用户主动发起；Sorta 不会持续追踪你的位置，也不会建立位置历史记录。

附近地点结果使用 Apple MapKit 和 Apple 地图服务。可使用大概位置，但结果可能不够精确。收藏地点名称可能存储在你的设备上；Sorta 不会将精确 GPS 历史作为笔记的一部分保存。

6. 通知与提醒

Sorta 使用 iOS 本地通知提供提醒。提醒送达可能取决于通知权限、设备设置、系统状态和 iOS 行为。Sorta 不保证提醒一定送达，也不应被用作紧急、医疗、法律或其他安全关键场景的提醒工具。

7. AI 功能

当前 Beta 中与 AI 相关的体验可能使用本地、规则式、模拟或演示逻辑。AI 整理和自动图片整理尚未全面开放。当前 Beta 不运营将你的完整笔记数据库发送给第三方 AI 服务商的生产流程。

“允许联网 AI”是为未来功能预留的控制项；打开它目前不会将你的笔记发送给 OpenAI、Gemini、Anthropic 或其他第三方 AI 服务商。若未来版本引入外部 AI 处理，我们会更新本政策，说明发送的数据、发送目的、涉及的服务商及适用的用户控制方式。

8. Apple 与系统服务

当你使用相关功能时，Apple 语音识别、Core Location、MapKit 与 Apple 地图、照片选择器、相机 API 和 UserNotifications 等 Apple 系统服务可能会依照 Apple 自己的技术和隐私做法处理信息。Sorta 无法控制 Apple 通过这些服务如何处理信息。

9. 数据共享

开发者不会出售用户的个人笔记数据。Sorta 目前不运营用于存储用户完整笔记数据库的服务器。

当前 Beta 不包含第三方广告或分析 SDK。

10. 数据保留与删除

本地数据通常会保留在你的设备上，直至你删除笔记、删除应用或清除相关应用数据。撤回只是短暂的应用内恢复机制，并非云端备份；开发者无法从服务器恢复你已删除的本地笔记。

11. 安全

Sorta 依赖 iOS 应用沙盒和设备级保护来保护本地应用数据。没有任何存储或传输方式是绝对安全的。

12. 儿童隐私

Sorta 是一款通用效率工具，并非专门面向儿童。

13. 本政策的变更

随着 Sorta 的发展，本政策可能会发生变化。若未来功能如云同步、外部 AI 处理、广告或分析会实质影响数据处理方式，我们将相应更新本政策。
